# Angular Material Palette Generator

This package lets you refine the color palettes to be integrated into Sass theme files generated by the Angular CLI
(`ng generate @angular/material:theme-color`).

Online demo: https://avine.github.io/angular-material-palette-generator

```scss
$_palettes: (
  primary: (
    // 👇 This tool helps you refine the following Sass map
    0: #000000,
    10: #001d33,
    20: #003354,
    25: #003e65,
    30: #004a77,
    35: #00568a,
    40: #00639d,
    50: #2f7cb8,
```

## How palettes are generated

A palette goes exactly through the colors you pin (the _anchors_) and every other tone is interpolated.

By default, a palette has a single anchor: its key color, pinned at its own tone.
You can pin as many colors as needed, at any tone (for example, your brand color at tone `40`, used by the
`--mat-sys-primary` token in light mode).

For each tone, the color is computed from 3 values:

- **Tone (lightness)**: as in Material Design 3, the tone of a color is its CIE L\* (from `0` for black to `100` for
  white). This is what makes the contrast between tokens predictable. When an anchor is pinned at a tone that does not
  match its own lightness, the lightness of the surrounding tones is smoothly shifted so that the palette goes through
  the anchor.
- **Saturation**: relative to the maximum saturation available in the sRGB gamut, and interpolated between anchors. All
  colors stay displayable and the saturation naturally decreases towards black and white.
- **Hue**: the OKLCH hue, interpolated between anchors along the shortest path on the color wheel.

The computation uses the OKLCH color space, but exported colors are hex colors.

## Usage

- **Pin a color**: click the pin icon next to a tone to pin its current color, then edit the color in the form. Click
  the pin icon again to unpin it. You can also add or remove colors in the form, and set their tone (leave the tone
  empty to pin a color at its own tone).
- **Inspect a color**: hover (or focus) the hex color of a tone to display its tone, saturation and hue.
- **Fix the issues**: the form lists the problems of the palette, for example two colors pinned at the same tone, or
  colors whose lightness is not in the same order as their tones.
- **Check the tokens**: each tone shows the Material tokens using it, for the selected color scheme (light or dark),
  and their WCAG contrast compliance.

## Light and dark palettes

Angular Material can use different palettes for light and dark modes (`theme-type: light` and `theme-type: dark`).
To tune each of them separately, work on one color scheme at a time: select the color scheme (light or dark), tune
the palettes by checking the tokens of this color scheme, then export them.
